Models on {{MAC}}

    Add one on the Mac: ollama pull qwen2.5:7b. It shows up here.

    Your Mac is the engine

    The model runs on {{MAC}}. This phone sends your message over your Wi-Fi and shows the answer as the Mac writes it. Nothing goes through a cloud, or through us.

    Put it on your Home Screen

    iPhone: Share, then Add to Home Screen. Android: the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen.

    Away from home

    berth has no relay of its own. Install Tailscale on the Mac and on this phone, sign both in to the same account, and open the Mac's Tailscale address with :7770 and the same code (berth serve prints it). The two devices connect directly; the Mac has to be awake.

    More models, or unpair

    On the Mac: ollama pull <model> adds a model. berth serve --new-token unpairs every phone.
